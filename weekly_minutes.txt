## First set of goals

Minimum viable product would be:
- No account creation (people play anonymously)
- Human vs human matchmaking *and* human vs AI
- Survey included post-game (even though poster fair survey would be paper and pen)
- Voice commands
- At least more than one piece animation option
- Some realistic move-time implementation
- App will be visitable by URL
- Only 1 AI opponent to choose from
